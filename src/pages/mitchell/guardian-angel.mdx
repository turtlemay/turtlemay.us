<pre class="prose">
A long time ago, with my newly opening eyes, I saw you.
Smiling.
Even in your greatest pain. You taught me to love and to pray. You loved me
when no one else did. From you, my comfort came with a gentle touch of a
cheek. Your eyes saw me as the greatest person who ever lived while
promising to be with me forever.
Weren't you my guardian angel? When you opened and fluttered your
embracing wings, it was beautiful.

... then once... in a dream vision, with closed eyes in silent undisturbed sleep, I
saw you... Fluttering once again, like a new butterfly waiting to take first flight.

...but soon then in that vision, I saw you swept suddenly skyward, soaring so
swiftly to mingle with a brightly, glowing shimmer of color, fluttering toward the
forever Heaven!

Then when I awoke,... once again...
I saw you.
Smiling:
Sweetly serene and still.
Forever frozen in eternal silence.

Your last words to me came forth from "nowhere" into my mind:
"I can't hear you, you're fading away..."
I called to find then, that you were gone.

...but now...
I still see you.
In my still and silent dreams, where we stroll our field, past the grove of wind-
whispering aspens, poplars and cottonwoods. Where you put me down so long
ago in a beautiful, green meadow to wipe my cheek and sigh... beneath the
very tree which I climbed high to "escape to silence" in childhood.

...and just this year,
I thought of your "escape." Then, I realized that the fluttering little butterfly who
sometimes silently follows me ... as I walk my trail, comforting me with her
colorful company, fluttering along and stopping with me in a field or meadow...
is YOU: ... my guardian angel still!
</pre>
