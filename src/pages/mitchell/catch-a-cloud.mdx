<div class="title">*"If I Could Catch a Cloud"* by Mitch</div>

<pre class="prose">
*If* I could catch a cloud, could I hold and fluff it as my pillow or squeeze it like a puffy teddy
bear? ..

*Hey...*
I might see if it tastes like cotton candy or maybe, I could thank it first for showing us those
fancy shapes and movements and for filtering the hot sun and for giving forth the rain...

*Or...*
I might tickle it to listen for a giggle, or see if it will let me rest on top of it for a while.

*Oh!*
What If I grasp within and touch an angel's wing?...

*And then...*
To set it free and on its way, I'll gently blow it through the sky to hear it sweetly
sigh "*goodbye*..."

<br />

<p><i>And so,... I'll look for it another day!</i></p>
</pre>
