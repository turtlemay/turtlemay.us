<div class="title">*"Waves"* by Mitch</div>

<pre class="prose">
The ocean sighs a soothing song ...
of gentle waves that play the tune.
You walk along ...
the surf who calls.

To bathe in time, the ancient past
surrounds you now.

The Ocean knows, that you belong,
but giant waves can take you down.
to mingle with the sand that's you.

Then sweetly bumped again to float,
you ride the youthful timely tide.
The smallest new waves pluck you up!

The pulse of life is all around,
it flows within you, up and down,
The people pulsate in and out ...
Like waves.

The ones you love have come then gone,
they wash upon you on and on,
and vanish as they fade away,
to slowly drift ... to open sea.

The waves you ride, the waves you are,
and those whose love is very far have
made you what you are today.

The ocean knows you can't remain,
it's lifeblood in you soon will drain,
to join the ever-pulsing sea.
</pre>
