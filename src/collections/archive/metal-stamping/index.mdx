![A stamped brass tag depicting a person's name surrounded by stars, connected to a chain ring.](./20171225_145649.jpg "A brass tag I made for a friend. I use a simple metal stamp set, age the tag in an acidic solution designed to add patina to metal, then sand off the highlights with high-grit leaving a nice contrast. Finally I attach it to a snap ball chain or keyring.")
