In honor of the eighth anniversary of my father's passing I wanted to share a few of his short poems that I found among his notes.

He was sixty years old at the time, always a talented storyteller, but I never knew him as a writer and was surprised to later find this handful of melancholic yet curiously uplifting poems in a coffee-stained stack of papers. I didn't care much for them back then but I hope you will find them as charming as I do today.
